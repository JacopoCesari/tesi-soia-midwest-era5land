# Excluded and Candidate Figures

This directory contains visual assets that are not currently included in the active thesis text (`output/thesis/chapters/`):

1. **`fig_detrending_diagnostics` (.pdf / .png)**:
   - Comprehensive 4-panel diagnostic plot comparing detrending specifications (Linear OLS, Quadratic, Cubic, Spline, HP Filter).
   - In Chapter 3 (Section 3.2), results are presented in tabular form via `tab_detrending_comparison.tex`. This diagnostic figure is preserved here for potential inclusion in Chapter 4 or a methodological appendix.

2. **`fig_weather_seasonality` (.pdf / .png)**:
   - Early exploratory version of the intra-annual agro-climatic seasonality plot.
   - Preserved as exploratory background.

3. **`fig_weather_climatology` (.pdf / .png)**:
   - Multi-decadal annual time series of heat days ($HD_{30}$) and summer $VPD$ synchronized with county yield anomalies (1950–2025).
   - Excluded to streamline Chapter 3: aggregate multi-decadal series diluted local compound shocks, which are far more rigorously demonstrated by the daily phenological trajectories (Figure 3.6 & 3.7), the spatial contrast (Figure 3.8), and the monthly sensitivity correlations (Figure 3.9).

4. **`fig_dispersion_dynamics` (.pdf / .png)**:
   - Early exploratory analysis of yield dispersion dynamics across the panel.
   - Superseded by the consolidated trajectory figures in Chapter 3.

5. **Spatial Shock Candidate Plots (`fig_spatial_shock_*`)**:
   - `fig_spatial_shock_option1_1988_1994.pdf` / `.png`: Option 1 candidate (1988 drought vs. 1994 bumper).
   - `fig_spatial_shock_option2_1993_2021.pdf` / `.png`: Option 2 candidate (1993 flood vs. 2021 bumper).
   - `fig_spatial_shock_option3_2012_2016.pdf` / `.png`: Option 3 candidate (2012 drought vs. 2016 bumper). Selected and adopted as the active `fig_spatial_shock_comparison.pdf` (Figure 3.9).
   - `fig_spatial_shock_comparison_optA.png`: Layout / palette option A draft.
   - `fig_spatial_shock_comparison_optB.png`: Layout / palette option B draft.
