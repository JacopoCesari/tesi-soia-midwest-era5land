# Sample selection rationale

Author decision recorded on 2026-09-15: prioritize the longest available yield
period, 1950–2025 inclusive, over the number of counties. This replaces the previous
1950–2010 primary panel. No model-performance comparison was used to make this choice.
The 135 counties complete over all 76 years provide 10,260 county-year observations.

The motivation is to observe more distinct crop years and support evaluation across
more temporal origins. Neighboring counties can share annual climatic conditions;
county-year row counts should therefore not be equated with independent information.
The dependence has not been quantified here. This is the author's design rationale,
not an empirical result or a method attributed to a particular paper.

The selected population remains conditional on the earlier 479-county export and
on uninterrupted reporting through 2025. It is not a claim of nationwide coverage
or a sample that could have been selected using only information available in 1950.
Selection changes geographic composition: the six states retain 28, 20, 35, 19, 6
and 27 counties, respectively (Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Minnesota, Missouri, Ohio).

The author recalls an earlier minimum-acreage filter. Its threshold and reference
period have not been recovered. Preserve this distinction between author-reported
provenance and reproducible filtering. This preparation applies no new acreage filter.

More years do not establish forecast superiority. The initial expanding-window
training length remains open, with no fixed 60/40 split. The complete target panel
also does not resolve pre-1950 weather needs or yield publication timing at early
forecast origins; these decisions must precede model fitting.
